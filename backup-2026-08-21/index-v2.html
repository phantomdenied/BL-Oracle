🔮 The Oracle — Help Guide

🚀 Getting started

The Oracle matches cards from your deck list against a Google Sheets inventory of singles for sale. Paste your card list, hit Search, select the copies you want, then generate your buy lists.

You can use The Oracle without signing in — signing in with Google only enables saving and loading your generated lists between sessions.

📋 Step by step
1
Select your TCG — use the dropdown in the top bar to choose MTG, Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh! or One Piece.
2
Paste your card list — one card per line, with or without quantities (4 Lightning Bolt or just Lightning Bolt).
3
Search — press Enter in the card box on desktop, or tap Search in the bottom bar.
4
Select copies — tick the specific prints you want, or use Auto-Pick.
5
Create Lists — generates a BL Buy List for inventory cards and a TCGplayer list for anything not found.
6
Copy — one tap on either Copy button puts the list on your clipboard.
🌙 Dark mode

The moon/sun button in the top bar toggles dark mode. It's independent of the TCG colour themes — every game works in both light and dark. Your choice is remembered, and if you've never chosen, The Oracle follows your device's appearance setting.

📥 Import Deck to Oracle bookmarklet

The bookmarklet imports a deck directly from Moxfield, MTGGoldfish or Archidekt in one click — no copy-pasting.

1
Make sure your browser's bookmarks bar is visible (Ctrl+Shift+B on Chrome/Firefox).
2
Find 📥 Import Deck to Oracle in the Cards to Search panel.
3
Drag it onto your bookmarks bar.
4
Open any deck page on Moxfield, MTGGoldfish or Archidekt and click the bookmark — you'll land back here with the list loaded.
📋 Manual import formats

If you prefer copy-paste, all of these work:

MoxfieldExport → Copy for Moxfield
MTGGoldfishExport to Arena → copy everything from the Commander section down

🔎 Match types
ExactThe card name matches exactly what's in the inventory.
PartialThe inventory has a version under a slightly different name — a different printing, alternate art, or the other face of a dual-faced card.
Not foundNo match in the inventory. These are added to your TCGplayer list automatically.
✦ Print types (MTG)

Each inventory copy is categorised by print type, shown as a corner triangle tucked under the card's name:

No triangle — standard regular print.
Silver triangle — hologram print (columns C & D).
Gold triangle — foil print (columns E & F).
🖼 Card images

On a phone, Show images in the results toolbar loads the actual card photo for every match the inventory has one for, filling the thumbnail slot on each row. Tap any thumbnail to see it full size. The setting is remembered between sessions.

On a larger screen, hover any match instead — the preview follows your cursor.

🎲 Auto-Pick (MTG only)

🎲 Random — one random available match per card, from any column.

✦ Modest — hologram prints only (columns C & D).

💎 Blinged — foil prints only (columns E & F).

Exact matches are always preferred. If no copy exists in the target columns, that card is skipped.

💾 Saved lists

Sign in with Google to save generated lists and reload them later from the dropdown at the top. Without signing in everything still works — the list just isn't kept when you leave. Saved lists live in this browser's local storage, so they're per-device.

💡 Tips

• Set codes and collector numbers are stripped automatically — Lightning Bolt (M10) 146 is treated as Lightning Bolt.

• *F* foil markers are stripped automatically.

• Use the filter buttons to focus on one result type — your filter is kept when you auto-pick.

• Hover a match on desktop to preview the card image, where the inventory has a Google Photos link.
